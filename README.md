# Inspiration Compass · prototype

The first step for leaders chosen to inspire others in **Leadership Inspiration**, Hilti's programme for experienced leaders.

In about six minutes, a leader recalls who inspired them, meets our Hilti Leadership Framework through that memory, shares their instinct and a moment they are proud of, and sees what they are a natural at passing on.

**Open it:** https://a-cash-ethz.github.io/inspiration-compass/

Add your first name to be greeted personally, for example `?name=Eivind`:
https://a-cash-ethz.github.io/inspiration-compass/?name=Eivind

---

## How to test it (about 6 minutes)

1. Open the link, ideally on your phone as well as your laptop.
2. Go through the seven chapters as if you had just been chosen as an inspirer.
3. At **Through others' eyes**, pick any three names and send. Example answers then fill in the result.
4. Read your result and open your **inspiration card**.
5. On **The year ahead**, tap each stage to see what follows.

You can stop at any time; it picks up where you left off.

## What we would value your view on

- **The first minute:** did the welcome make you want to continue?
- **Who inspired you:** did the framework make sense when it appeared?
- **The three dilemmas:** do they feel real for your part of Hilti?
- **The result:** does "what you will pass on" feel true, or generic?
- **Overall:** would a leader with ten years in role feel recognised, or assessed?

One sentence on each is plenty.

## The other two views

- Own leader: https://a-cash-ethz.github.io/inspiration-compass/#leader

## Good to know

- **It is a prototype.** Team answers, invitations, the Workday names and the fellow inspirers are examples.
- **Nothing you type leaves your browser.** Progress is saved on your own device only, so you can resume. No data is collected.
- **Light mode, any device.** Works in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox.
- **Not yet in the pilot.** The 2027 pilot uses the Leadership Circle 360; the Compass is proposed for when the programme scales.
